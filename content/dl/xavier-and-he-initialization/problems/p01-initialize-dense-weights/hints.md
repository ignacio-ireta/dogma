## Hint 1 — concept

Choose a variance target before choosing a distribution. The two schemes account for different activation behavior.

## Hint 2 — formula / approach

Uniform(-a,a) has variance a*a/3; a normal with standard deviation s has variance s*s.

## Hint 3 — code shape

Validate arguments before constructing a local generator. Derive the scale and make exactly the specified draw.
