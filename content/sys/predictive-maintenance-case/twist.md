# Sensor swap

After the candidate's first structure: in week 27, field crews replaced the vibration sensors at five of the
nine sites with a newer model that reports on a different scale. Nobody told the data team. Over the next
three weeks the weekly alert list grew from about 10 compressors to about 30, and the first 40 inspections
on those sites found no fault. How does this change your plan, and what would you check first?
