# Predictive maintenance system

A plant operator runs 1,200 rotary compressors across nine sites. Each compressor is overhauled on a
fixed twelve-month cycle. Between overhauls, compressors still fail without warning, and a failure stops
production for more than four hours. Each unplanned failure costs money in parts, emergency labour and lost
output, and a stopped line delays customer orders.

Every compressor reports vibration, bearing temperature, motor current and discharge pressure. The operator
has three years of these readings, along with the work-order and overhaul records.

The operations director wants a weekly list of compressors that are likely to fail soon, so that crews can
inspect and repair them before they stop. The data team has been asked to build it.

How would you build the system, and how would you know it is worth running?
