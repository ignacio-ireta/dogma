# Card fraud

A card issuer processes 50 million card transactions a month. Fraud losses doubled from 8 to 16 million
dollars a month, mostly in online purchases. The current rules catch about 60 percent of fraud, but they
flag so many transactions that investigators review only 30 percent of the alerts. The rest are approved
automatically.

Design a detection system that catches more fraud without overwhelming the review team. A decision has to
come back within 200 milliseconds.

How would you build it?
