# The popularity loop

After the candidate's first structure: the team launches a personalised ranker to half of the traffic. Four
weeks later, purchases per session are up 2.9 percent, from 2.10 to 2.16 per 100 sessions. But the share of
feed impressions going to the top 1 percent of listings has risen from 18 to 41 percent. The number of
listings that sold at least once in a week fell by 9 percent. New-listing sell-through, the share of new
listings with a sale within 14 days, fell from 22 to 13 percent, and the new-seller share of impressions is
now 7 percent. What does this change in your plan, and what do you do this week?
