# Marketplace home feed

A mid-size online marketplace for home goods has 2.4 million monthly active buyers and 900,000 active
listings. About 60,000 new listings arrive each week. Buyers make about 1.6 million sessions a week, and
2.1 in every 100 sessions end in a purchase.

The home feed shows every buyer the same list, sorted by sales in the last 14 days. The product team wants
a personal feed for the home page and the "similar items" panel. Leadership wants more purchases per
session, and it does not want the marketplace's independent sellers to lose their share of attention.

How would you build the feed, and how would you know it is better than the list it replaces?
