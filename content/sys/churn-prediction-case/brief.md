# Mobile churn

A mid-size mobile operator has 12 million subscribers on prepaid and postpaid plans. Monthly churn rose
from 2.1 to 3.4 percent over the last 18 months. The industry average is 2.5 percent.

Acquiring a subscriber costs about 120 dollars, and keeping one costs about 30. Leadership wants to know
why churn is rising, and wants a system that finds at-risk subscribers early enough to keep them.
You have three years of data: demographics, usage, billing, support contacts and contract terms.

How would you build it?
