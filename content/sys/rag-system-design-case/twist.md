# The restricted page and the old runbook

After the candidate's first structure: an audit of the two-week pilot, covering 9,000 questions, finds three
answers that quoted a compensation review page. That page was restricted to HR 19 hours before those answers
were given. A fourth answer cited a runbook that had been superseded six weeks earlier, and an engineer followed
its steps in production. Nobody was notified. How does this change the design, and what do you check before the
pilot widens?
