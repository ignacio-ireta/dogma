# Internal policy assistant

A 4,000-person company keeps about 1.2 million internal documents in six systems: a wiki, a ticketing tool, a
shared drive, a policy repository, a runbook library and an HR portal. Each system has its own access rules,
and about 8 percent of documents are restricted to a few groups.

The support desk gets about 2,000 questions a working day. Many are answered by someone searching for the
right page. A question that escalates takes a support agent a median of 14 minutes. Leadership wants employees
to get answers from these documents, with a link to the source for every claim.

Design an assistant that answers from the documents. How would you know it is ready to launch?
