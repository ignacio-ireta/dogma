# Depot B closes

After the candidate's first structure: at 06:30 on dispatch day, depot B loses power and is closed for the day.
Its trucks are not loaded yet. At the same time, zone 4 orders arrive 25 percent above the evening forecast, from
240 to 300 pallets. You have one re-plan, and it must be ready within 15 minutes. What does the plan look like now,
how much can it change, and what do you tell the dispatchers?
