## Timeline

- **2023:** Moonshot AI founded by Yang Zhilin (ex-Google Brain).
- **2024:** Kimi Chat launches. Initially notable for 200K context window.
- **Late 2024:** Kimi extends to 2M tokens. Can process entire novels, codebases, financial reports in one prompt.
- **2025:** Kimi k1.5, k2 — reasoning and multimodal capabilities.

## Key Innovation

**Long context as a product:** While others compete on reasoning benchmarks, Kimi's bet is that *context length* is the unlock. If you can fit an entire codebase, legal document, or research corpus in one prompt, you don't need RAG — you just read.

## Why It Matters

Kimi represents the "narrow but deep" approach — one capability pushed to its limit (long context) rather than competing across all benchmarks. It's a reminder that the AI landscape isn't a single race; it's many parallel races, and different models win different ones.
