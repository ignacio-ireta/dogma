## Timeline

- **Nov 2022:** ChatGPT launches (GPT-3.5 + RLHF). Fastest-growing consumer app in history — 100M users in 2 months.
- **Mar 2023:** GPT-4 released. Multimodal (text + image input), significantly more capable. "The model that made AI credible."
- **Nov 2023:** GPT-4 Turbo, 128K context, cheaper, knowledge cutoff updates.
- **May 2024:** GPT-4o — natively multimodal (text, vision, audio), 2x faster, 50% cheaper.
- **Sep 2024:** o1 ("Strawberry") — chain-of-thought reasoning model. Thinks before answering. Math, code, science leap.
- **Dec 2024:** o3 announced. ARC-AGI benchmark breakthrough.
- **2025:** GPT-4.1, o3/o4-mini — continuing the dual track (fast + reasoning).

## Why It Matters

ChatGPT is the reason AI is on every executive's agenda. The product itself is less interesting than what it *caused*: every tech company building LLMs, every enterprise adopting AI, every regulator scrambling to understand.

## Controversy

- Training data opacity
- Nonprofit → capped-profit → effectively for-profit transition
- "Open" in name only — models are proprietary
- Safety team departures (Jan Leike, Ilya Sutskever)
