## Timeline

- **Aug 2023:** Qwen-7B. First release. Chinese + English bilingual.
- **Sep 2023:** Qwen-14B, 72B. Scaling up.
- **2024:** Qwen 1.5, Qwen 2, Qwen 2.5 — rapid iteration. Multiple sizes (0.5B–72B).
- **Jan 2025:** Qwen 2.5-Max — competitive with GPT-4 class. MoE variants.
- **Mar 2025:** Qwen 3 — state-of-art multilingual, thinking models.

## Key Strengths

- **Multilingual by design:** Not English-first with translation. Trained on Chinese + English + 30+ languages natively.
- **Coding:** Qwen-Coder models consistently top open-weight coding benchmarks.
- **Math:** Qwen-Math competitive with specialized math models.
- **Size variety:** Every scale from 0.5B (edge) to 72B+ (frontier), all open-weight.

## Technical Lineage

- **Architecture:** Transformer decoder with RoPE, SwiGLU, GQA (grouped-query attention)
- **MoE variants:** Sparse models for efficiency
- **Training:** Massive multilingual corpus (web + curated Chinese data)

## Why It Matters

Qwen represents China's AI capability at the frontier. For the lineage, it's important because it shows that Transformer + data + compute = frontier regardless of origin. The architecture is universal. The data is local. The capability is global.
