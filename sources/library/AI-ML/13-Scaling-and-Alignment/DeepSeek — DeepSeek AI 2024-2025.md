## Timeline

- **2024:** DeepSeek-V2 — MoE architecture, efficient inference.
- **Dec 2024:** DeepSeek-V3 — 671B total params, 37B active per token. Trained on 14.8T tokens for $5.576M (claimed). Sent shockwaves through the industry.
- **Jan 2025:** DeepSeek-R1 — reasoning model competitive with OpenAI o1, open-weight. The model that crashed Nvidia's stock.

## Key Innovations

1. **MoE efficiency:** Only 37B of 671B parameters active per token. Massive capacity, manageable compute.
2. **Training cost:** V3 trained for ~$5.6M — orders of magnitude cheaper than GPT-4 class training runs. Proved that frontier capability doesn't require frontier budgets.
3. **R1 reasoning:** Distilled chain-of-thought capability. Demonstrated that reasoning can emerge from smaller models with the right training.
4. **Multi-head Latent Attention (MLA):** Efficient attention mechanism that reduces KV cache.

## Why It Matters

DeepSeek is the China Efficiency Story. When US export controls limited access to high-end GPUs, DeepSeek responded by innovating on *efficiency* — better architectures, better training recipes, better utilization. The constraint became the catalyst.

R1's release in January 2025 was a Sputnik moment: open-weight reasoning models competitive with the best proprietary ones, from a company most people hadn't heard of.
