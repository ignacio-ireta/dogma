# Mock Interview Scoring Rubric

*For mock interviewers. Score each section after the interview, then debrief together.*

---

## How to Use

Score each item 1-4:
- **1** = Did not demonstrate
- **2** = Attempted but weak
- **3** = Solid, meets expectations
- **4** = Exceptional, would impress a real interviewer

### Interpretation
- **90-120:** Strong. Ready for the real thing.
- **70-89:** Solid foundation, specific areas to improve.
- **50-69:** Needs more practice. Identify the weakest section and drill it.
- **Below 50:** Early stage. Focus on RASS framework and model justification basics.

---

## Debrief Questions (ask these after scoring)

1. "What did you think went well?"
2. "Where did you feel stuck?"
3. "If you could redo one part, what would you change?"
4. Give your **top 1 strength** and **top 1 area to improve** — be specific.
