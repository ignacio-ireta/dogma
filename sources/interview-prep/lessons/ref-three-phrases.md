# Three Phrases — Memorize These

Use at least one during the interview. They signal consulting-ready thinking.

---

## Phrase 1: Before Model Selection

> "Before choosing a model, I want to understand what decision this model needs to support and how the output will be used."

**When to use:** Right after the interviewer presents the case, before you jump to any technical solution.

---

## Phrase 2: Start Simple

> "I'd start with a simple baseline — logistic regression or CatBoost with defaults — to establish a performance floor, then increase complexity only if the business case justifies the additional development time."

**When to use:** When they ask "what model would you use?"

---

## Phrase 3: Deployment Reality

> "The model accuracy matters less than whether the organization can actually act on the predictions. A simpler model that the operations team trusts and uses will outperform a complex model that sits unused."

**When to use:** During synthesis, or when pushed on model complexity vs interpretability.

---

*These aren't scripts — they're principles expressed as sentences. Rephrase naturally. The IDEAS matter more than the exact words.* 🏔️
